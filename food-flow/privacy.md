# Privacy Policy — Food Flow: Pixel Puzzle

**Last updated:** October 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Sisyphus Games ("we", "us") handles information when you play
**Food Flow: Pixel Puzzle** (the "Game") on Android.

## Summary

- The Game does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email, or other contact
  details.
- Your game progress is stored on your device.
- The Game may use third-party analytics services to understand how players use the Game and to
  improve it. These services collect device and usage data, as described below.
- We do not sell your personal information.

## Information stored on your device

The Game saves your progress (levels completed, coins, boosters, settings) locally on your device so
that you can continue where you left off. This data is deleted when you clear the Game's data or
uninstall the Game.

## Information collected by analytics

The Game may use analytics services provided by third parties ("service providers"). When you play,
they may automatically collect:

- **Device information:** device model, operating system and version, language, country/region,
  screen size.
- **Identifiers:** an app-specific or installation identifier and, where permitted by your device
  settings, your device's advertising ID (Google Advertising ID).
- **Usage data:** game events such as sessions, levels started, completed or failed, boosters used,
  time played, and technical data such as crashes and performance.
- **Network information:** IP address, used to estimate approximate location (country or region)
  and for security.

We use this information to:

- understand how players progress through the Game and balance its difficulty;
- find and fix bugs, crashes and performance problems;
- measure the effectiveness of our marketing.

Service providers process this information on our behalf and under their own privacy policies.
The service providers currently used by the Game are listed in the "Service providers" section below.

The Game does not collect your name, email address, contacts, precise location, photos, or files.

## Advertising ID

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization at any time in your device
settings (on most Android devices: **Settings → Google → Ads** or **Settings → Privacy → Ads**).
Once you delete it, apps receive no advertising ID at all.

## Legal basis (EEA, UK)
Where the GDPR applies, we process analytics data on the basis of our legitimate interest in
operating and improving the Game, or on your consent where consent is required by law. Where we
rely on consent, you can withdraw it at any time.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR or the CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right
to access, correct or delete personal data about you, to object to or restrict its processing, and
to data portability. To make a request, contact us at the address below. We may ask for information
needed to locate your data (for example, your device's advertising ID).

## Data retention

Analytics data is kept only as long as necessary for the purposes above, after which it is deleted
or anonymized. Data stored on your device remains until you clear the Game's data or uninstall it.

## Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country), and
we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided us with
personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

## Security

We and our service providers use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect
information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.
